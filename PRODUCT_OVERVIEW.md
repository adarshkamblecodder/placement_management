# Executive Product Overview: Campus Placement Management Platform

## Executive Summary & Problem Statement

For decades, Training & Placement Officers (TPOs) and college placement cells have managed seasonal recruitment campaigns across hundreds of graduating candidates and dozens of visiting corporate recruiters using disconnected spreadsheets, manual email chains, and ad-hoc WhatsApp groups.

### What Fails in Traditional Spreadsheet Placements?
1. **Manual Eligibility Cross-Checking**: Verifying whether each student meets specific criteria (minimum CGPA, active backlogs, eligible branches) takes days of manual cross-referencing.
2. **Offer Conflict & "Double Placement" Chaos**: When a student receives an offer, manually tracking and withdrawing them from other companies' in-progress interview pipelines leads to offer hoarding and wasted corporate slots.
3. **Data Discrepancy & Unverified Resumes**: Students submitting outdated GPA numbers or unverified credentials risk damaging the institution's corporate credibility.
4. **Opaque Leadership Reporting**: Generating presentable reports for College Principals, Directors, and Accreditation Boards (NAAC/NBA) requires days of spreadsheet formatting.

## 🎯 Target Personas

| Persona | Primary Needs & Jobs to Be Done |
|---|---|
| **Training & Placement Officer (TPO)** | Drive lifecycle management, automated student eligibility screening, shortlisting candidates, company relationship tracking (CRM), bulk announcements, and leadership analytics. |
| **Department Placement Coordinator** | Scoped management of students in their specific branch (e.g. CS, AIML), profile verification, and interview attendance monitoring. |
| **Student Candidate** | Centralized dashboard for applying to eligible drives, resume management with auto-skill extraction, interview schedule tracking, and offer acceptance. |
| **College Leadership (Principal/Dean)** | Real-time placement rates, branch-wise comparison, median/highest package distributions, and exportable Excel reports. |

---

## 🚀 Core Value Pillars

### 1. Zero-Manual Eligibility Automation
TPOs configure drive criteria (Min CGPA, Max Backlogs, Branches, Year). The platform automatically screens all candidates in real time. Ineligible students receive transparent, constructive feedback explaining the criteria shortfall.

### 2. Automated One-Offer Conflict Resolution
Enforces customizable college placement policies (*Strict One-Offer*, *Dream Tier Exception*, or *Multiple Offers*). When a student accepts an offer, the system automatically withdraws their active candidacies across all other companies, instantly opening opportunities for unplaced peers.

### 3. Corporate CRM & Relationship History
Maintains institutional memory on corporate recruiters: historical campus visits, offered packages, hiring volume trends, and internal TPO evaluation ratings.

### 4. Verified Candidate Profiles
Introduces a profile verification gate ensuring that candidate academic metrics and resumes are reviewed by coordinators before appearing on recruiter shortlists.

### 5. Multi-College White-Label Ready
Custom college branding, logo fallbacks, dynamic accent themes, and customizable "Powered by" credits ready for multi-tenant institutional deployment.
